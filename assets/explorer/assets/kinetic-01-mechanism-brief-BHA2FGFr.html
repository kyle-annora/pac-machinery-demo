Illustrative sample

One rotary input. A guided linear output.

01 / INPUT

The crank turns

An offset pin follows the rotating crank around one complete revolution.

02 / TRANSFER

The rod changes angle

The connecting rod follows the crank pin and transfers movement to the carriage.

03 / OUTPUT

The carriage travels

Two fixed rails constrain the carriage as it moves outward, inward and back.
