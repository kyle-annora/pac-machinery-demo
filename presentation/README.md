# Annora × PAC Machinery — formal pitch

15 scenes, an independent English speaker script, Patrick’s shared discovery example and preserved search evidence, three future-demo entry pages and four editable diagrams.

## Open

- `index.html`: audience presentation. Use arrows or Space to reveal the next point. Contents jumps to a scene. Present hides the controls; Escape returns. Full screen appears at the lower-right in presentation mode.
- `notes.html`: English narration and scene bridges, separate from the audience view. Follow slides synchronizes with the open deck on the same origin.
- `demo.html`: Website, Customer proposal and Sales Workspace plans. These are explicit placeholders, not completed artifacts.
- `evidence.html`: Patrick’s shared ChatGPT medical-packaging example, the proposed AI / SEO delivery plan, two complementary Perplexity searches and official PAC configuration sources.
- `journey.html`: buyer/seller journey, issue/publish boundary, A/B revision relationships, and trial decision diagrams.

The deck uses a fixed 1920 × 1080 stage that scales uniformly. Desktop or a landscape display is recommended for presenting; supporting documents reflow on phones.

## Evidence and commercial boundaries

- Patrick’s shared ChatGPT medical-packaging answer names four suppliers without PAC. This is one observation; PAC appears in our complementary searches. The medical discovery example is separate from the hardware-kit quoting case.
- AI / SEO trial work: agreed buyer-question baseline, source-linked observations, prioritized changes, one application-page draft and a measurement plan. Publishing and continued implementation are included only within agreed scope.
- Two independently submitted Perplexity queries, signed out, captured on November 8, 2026. This is exploratory evidence, not a ranking study or evidence of improved acquisition. Original sessions may expire; preserved screenshots remain available.
- The PAC R3200 official product page supports the Easy Load Bag Opener / Seal Flattener Fingers dependency and minimum 10-inch bag width. Application fit, current prices and lead time require PAC confirmation.
- Harbor Components is illustrative. Product photographs are model/option references, not an issued quote configuration.
- Proposed trial: two weeks free, with scope and review cases agreed before starting. Paid operation requires mutual agreement on scope, fees and support. No automatic charges.
