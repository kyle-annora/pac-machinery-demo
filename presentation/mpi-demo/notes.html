Presenter controls

  1. Start only after the model is ready.
  2. Pause with the visible button, Space or Escape. Moving the model or hiding this tab also pauses the sequence.
  3. Resume continues at the same scene and time. Restart is available while paused or after completion.
  4. Return to the customer outcomes slide at the end. It remains immediately before the trial offer.
