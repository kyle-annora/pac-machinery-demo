What this example establishes

We can make a complex product easier to inspect, explain a prepared movement and focus a conversation on a specific area. A PAC version begins with an approved, usable PAC equipment model and agreed content.

The study retains all 139 core source meshes and the original moving-platen group. It is not manufacturer CAD, a PAC model, a verified machine configuration, or evidence that MPI has deployed this Explorer. The six-second motion is authored presentation timing; it does not establish cycle time, clearance or mechanical safety.

Native engineering finishes differ from the earlier MPI rendering. Guards can appear opaque; the tour uses native isolation to reveal the working area. The untextured model omits two image appearances: the original MPI logo decal and control-screen bitmap. Their original files remain preserved in the source package; no replacement artwork is invented.

MPI marks and imagery retain their owners’ rights. No general open-source license is asserted for this model or media. Three.js is separately MIT licensed. The source package records the geometry snapshot, transformations and asset hashes.
