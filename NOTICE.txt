Retain the complete assets/explorer/LICENSES.txt, all companion license files and model attribution files with redistributed copies. Those notices identify applicable licenses, authors and source references.

The default Kinetic 01 crank-slider mechanism is by Pradeep Kaushik and is a reference demonstration model, not manufacturer CAD. Its source and MIT notice are included with the Explorer model files.

Source Sans 3 and JetBrains Mono are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their complete license notices and the font-file provenance manifest are included in assets/fonts/.

The public runtime catalog manifest is a projection of the supplied catalog manifest: run_id, status, signed_off and catalog_sha256. The hash identifies the original signed-off source catalog; it is not a checksum of the reduced manifest or this repository. Runtime catalog records and media are copied from that source without changing product or pricing values.
